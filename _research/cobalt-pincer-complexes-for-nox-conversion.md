## Research focus

I am an undergraduate student researcher in the Inorganic Molecular Conversion
Laboratory at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Yunho Lee.

My work involves synthesizing PNP pincer ligands and analogous cobalt complexes
as platforms for open-shell $\mathrm{NO}_x$ conversion and upconversion into
value-added organic compounds.
