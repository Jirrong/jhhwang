## Research focus

During Advanced R&E in Chemistry at Gyeonggi Science High School, I studied
polarity conversion using implicit-solvation models and conformational
fluctuations in a sterically constrained molecule.

## Methods

- Implicit-solvation models
- Conformational analysis
- Excited-state calculations using time-dependent density functional theory (TD-DFT)
  and equation-of-motion coupled-cluster (EOM-CC) methods
