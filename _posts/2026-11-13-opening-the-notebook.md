This blog is a place for notes on chemistry: questions from papers, explanations of concepts, and records of calculations or methods.

<!--more-->

## Topics

My interests include organic synthesis, reaction mechanisms, and computational chemistry. Posts may take the form of a paper discussion, a worked derivation, a short programming note, or a question that needs further investigation.

## References and discussion

For technical notes, I aim to include references and the conditions needed to understand or reproduce an example. A paper's results and my interpretation of them should be distinguishable.
